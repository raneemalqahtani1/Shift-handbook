Leadership & working structureHow leadership is organized

SHIFT's leadership runs in five levels: the Faculty Advisor, the President, the Vice President, the Directors, and the Leads. Every lead owns a clear area, and the whole team works on four values: ownership, accountability, collaboration, and continuous improvement. Roles are distinct; success is collective.

Show full detailsHide details

Core values

  • Ownership: every lead owns a clear area and is accountable for progress within it.
  • Accountability: commitments, deadlines, communication, and documentation matter.
  • Collaboration: cross-team work is expected; departments coordinate rather than work in silos.
  • Continuous improvement: teams review what worked, what did not, and what should change next.

Meetings & communicationWeekly rhythm and meeting standards

Executives and directors sync every Monday, departments meet mid-week, and the whole club gathers once a month. Agendas go out 24 hours ahead, Operations records minutes and action items, and open items carry over until they are done.

Show full detailsHide details

Weekly meeting structure

WhenMeetingWho
MondayExecutive & directors sync: progress, budget, milestones, and blockersPresident, VP, Directors
Wednesday / ThursdayDepartment meetings: delegate tasks, track deliverables, share updatesLeads and their teams
MonthlyAll-hands gathering: celebrate milestones, project updates, upcoming eventsAll members

Meeting standards

  • Agenda sharing: agendas are shared 24 hours before any meeting through official channels.
  • Action items & minutes: Operations tracks minutes, decisions, and action items with deadlines.
  • Accountability: leads report on key indicators and follow through on commitments.
  • Decision log: decisions affecting several teams are documented and shared.
  • Follow-up: open items are reviewed at the next meeting until completed or reassigned.

Every meeting, three steps

  1. Prepare: review the agenda and bring updates, questions, and blockers.
  2. Participate: contribute clearly, listen actively, focus on decisions.
  3. Deliver: complete action items by the deadline and report progress.

Team operating principlesHow SHIFT works together

Five principles guide daily work: one shared direction, clear ownership of every task, raising blockers early, keeping progress documented, and designing every activity so members grow through hands-on experience.

Show full detailsHide details
  • One direction: each department has its own responsibilities, but all work supports the same vision.
  • Clear ownership: tasks get an owner and a deadline and are followed through to completion.
  • Open communication: blockers are raised early, not after a deadline is missed.
  • Documented progress: decisions, meeting outcomes, and project information stay accessible.
  • Student growth: activities are designed to develop members, not only to produce outcomes.

Community rulesConduct, safety, and accountability

Eight rules keep the club safe and fair. More than three unexcused absences can lead to a membership review, and three inactive weeks may count as withdrawal. Equipment must be logged, safety procedures followed, and harassment or dishonest work is never accepted.

Show full detailsHide details
  • Attendance & punctuality: more than three unexcused absences may lead to a role or membership review.
  • Active participation: members contribute to an approved activity; three consecutive inactive weeks without approval may be treated as voluntary withdrawal.
  • Equipment accountability: components and tools are logged out and back in; damage or loss is reported immediately; nothing leaves without authorization.
  • Deadlines: respect sprint commitments, task deadlines, and competition milestones.
  • Professional communication: use KU email and approved channels; public posts representing SHIFT need approval.
  • Academic integrity: plagiarism, unauthorized copying, or dishonest technical work is not acceptable.
  • Respect & inclusion: discrimination, harassment, bullying, and disrespect are not tolerated.
  • Safety: follow workspace, electronics, tools, and robotics safety procedures at all times.

VEX coachWho we're looking for

SHIFT is looking for a coach with proven VEX experience who can teach as well as build, train the team from the start of prep through competition day, and help students build, program, drive, and document a competitive robot themselves.

Show full detailsHide details

Must-have

  • Verifiable VEX V5RC or VEX U experience as competitor, coach, or mentor; VEX U is best.
  • Can explain building, programming, and strategy to students at different levels.
  • Technical depth in mechanics (drivetrains, lifts, intakes) and programming (C++, Python, PROS, or VEXcode).
  • Knows the Override game and rules, or can master the game manual including the VEX U section.
  • Knows the engineering notebook and judging interview process.
  • A fixed weekly schedule (suggested two 2–3 hour sessions a week, more before events) and presence on competition day.

Nice to have

  • Experience at Worlds or a Signature Event.
  • Strategy and scouting experience.
  • Fluent Arabic or English, ideally both.
  • Experience running practice schedules and managing a small team.
